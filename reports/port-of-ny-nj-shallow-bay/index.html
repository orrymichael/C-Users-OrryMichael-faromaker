From the dock to the dock door

Rising port throughput doesn't stay at the terminal — it cascades into last-mile distribution demand across the state and the wider Northeast region. Shallow bay, infill product is the format built to absorb it.

1Port volume climbs

8.9M TEU in 2025, imports up 1.7% to 4.5M TEU despite tariff uncertainty.

2Freight fans out regionally

Cargo moves off-port via the NJ Turnpike corridor and rail (+12.4% YoY) into distribution nodes statewide.

3Big-box supply can't flex

Bulk buildings >500K SF run 8.1% vacant; 3PL and e-commerce users need smaller, closer-in space fast.

4Shallow bay absorbs it

Small-bay product (<100K SF) sits at 4.8% vacancy — the tightest, most durable format in the region.

Why the format wins

Two data points explain the thesis: vacancy is structurally tighter in shallow bay than in any other format, and rent holds firmest in the submarkets closest to the port.
